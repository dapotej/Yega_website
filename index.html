The thinking behind YEGA

I’ve spent years inside the acquisition systems of the world’s largest organisations. The gap is significant.

At Microsoft, William Hill and GIMO, I saw how engineered acquisition systems produce predictable, scalable revenue. At most professional service firms I’ve spoken to, I see the opposite — skilled, capable people operating on relationships and instinct, with no visibility into what’s working and no system to make it consistent.

That gap is entirely fixable. Not by replacing what works — but by connecting it, measuring it and engineering it into something that produces a predictable pipeline independent of any one partner.

YEGA is the firm I wished existed when I was on the inside of those acquisition functions. We don’t sell marketing. We engineer the systems behind client acquisition.
